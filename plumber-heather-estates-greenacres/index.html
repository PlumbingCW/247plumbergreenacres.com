What we know about Heather Estates

Community directories describe Heather Estates as a non-gated community of 58 homes on Heather Dr E and Heather Dr W. A City of Greenacres park sits at 3851 Heather Dr W, in the 33463 ZIP code. Those are the local facts this page relies on.

We have not verified the year the homes were built, the pipe materials, or whether each home is on sewer or septic. We would rather say so than guess. If any of that matters for your job, the quickest way to settle it is to ask the utility about your address and let us look inside.

A small community is easy to describe over the phone, which helps. Tell us the street, the house number and what you see, and we can usually tell you what to do first.

East and west are different streets

Heather Dr E and Heather Dr W are listed as two streets, so the direction is part of the address. When you call, say which one you are on, along with the house number. That small detail keeps a visit from starting on the wrong block.

It also helps when more than one home on the same street has the same problem. A single slow drain points to the house. Slow drains and low pressure at several neighbors' homes point to something shared, and that is a question for the utility. Our drain cleaning service covers the house side of the line.

General guidance, not a claim about specific homes

The sections below describe common plumbing problems in any Florida home. We have no verified information on the condition of plumbing in Heather Estates houses.

Signs that deserve a call

A hose bib that drips all day

A small drip adds up and is easy to fix. Left alone, it can soak the soil near the foundation.

A toilet that runs after flushing

A worn flapper or fill valve wastes water around the clock. A toilet repair is quick and low cost.

Gurgling drains and a damp yard patch

Together they can point to a partial blockage in the main line. A sewer camera inspection shows what is wrong without digging.

Hot water that runs out too fast

A tank that heats slowly or runs out sooner than it used to may be near the end of its life. See water heater repair first, then replacement if the tank is failing.

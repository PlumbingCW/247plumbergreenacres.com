Before we arrive

Four things to do while you wait for a plumber

You do not need tools for any of this. These steps limit damage and give the plumber a cleaner starting point.

Shut off the water

Turn the main shutoff valve clockwise until it stops. Most homes have it near the water meter or where the line enters the house. Open a faucet at the lowest point to drain what is left.

Cut power near water

If water is near outlets, appliances or the electrical panel, switch off those breakers only if you can reach them while standing on dry ground. Otherwise stay out and tell us when you call.

Stop using drains

For a backup, stop flushing and running sinks, tubs and the washing machine. Every gallon you add has nowhere to go but the floor.

Photograph the damage

Take clear photos of the leak, the wet areas and any damaged items before you move things. Your insurer and your plumber will both want them.

Greenacres

Plumbing in a city of about 44,000 residents

Greenacres covers roughly six square miles with 17,108 dwelling units, according to the City, and traffic moves along Lake Worth Road, Forest Hill Boulevard, Jog Road and Tenth Avenue North. That mix of homes, shops and plazas means we see everything from a kitchen drain in a house near Ira Van Bullock Park to a grease-clogged line at a restaurant along Jog Road. Commercial plumbing calls follow the same rules as home calls: clear price first, then the work.

Most of the city is supplied by the Palm Beach County Water Utilities Department, which draws from the Biscayne Aquifer and disinfects with chloramine. The City's own planning document encourages converting wells and septic tanks to centralized service, and existing septic systems may stay until centralized service reaches the property.

If you are not sure whether your house is on septic or sewer, ask the utility before a drain problem forces the question. A sewer camera inspection will also tell us what we are dealing with.

Water-heater changeouts in Greenacres go through the building department. Like-for-like swaps use the Palm Beach County Universal Permit Application and a Water Heater Changeout Form, and tankless units need a plan check. We explain the paperwork on the water heater replacement page so you know what to expect before the old tank comes out.

Pricing

How plumbing prices get set

We do not post exact prices online because a leak under a slab and a leak under a sink are not the same job. What we do is tell you the price before work starts, once we know what is wrong. Access, pipe material, parts, permits, time of day and how much cleanup is involved are the usual drivers.

If water damage is part of the picture, insurance may or may not respond. Policies treat sudden failures and slow leaks differently, so read your policy and call your insurer early. Keep photos and any parts we replace until the claim is settled.

Where the code fits in

Plumbing work in Florida follows the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which Florida builds on the International Plumbing Code (IPC). It is the rulebook behind how drains are vented, how supply lines are sized and how fixtures are connected.

For anything beyond a simple repair, ask the City of Greenacres building department whether a permit applies. Permit applications there are accepted only electronically.

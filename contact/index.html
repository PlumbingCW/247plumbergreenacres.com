What to tell us when you call

A few details let us give you better first steps and a clearer picture of the visit.

Where you are

Give the street address or cross streets and the town. If you are near Palm Springs or Wellington, say so.

What you see

Describe the water, the smell, the sound or the fixture. Mention whether more than one drain is affected.

What you already did

Tell us if the main valve is off, if power is off in wet areas, and if anything has been taken apart.

The property type

House, townhome, rental or business, and whether you own it or manage it for someone else.

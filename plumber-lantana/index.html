Whose problem is it? The town utility boundary and your plumbing

Lantana runs a town-owned water utility. The Town describes its service area as east of I-95 and north of Hypoluxo Road, extending until about one mile north of Lantana Road. The utility office can be reached at 561-540-5020.

That boundary matters because a Lantana address does not prove you are on the town system. If you are not sure who sends your water bill, look at it or call the utility and ask whether your street is inside the service area.

On our side, everything inside the property is a plumbing job: shutoff valves, supply lines, pressure regulators, water heaters and fixtures. In most systems the meter marks the handoff, but ask the utility to confirm where its side ends at your address before anyone digs.

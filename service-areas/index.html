Who to ask about the water system

When water trouble starts outside the house, the utility may be the right first call. Inside the house, it is a plumber. This table lists who runs the system in each town, using what each town publishes.

A leak between the meter and the house, or anywhere inside it, is a plumbing job. A broken main in the street or a boil water notice comes from the utility, and you follow its instructions.

Distance and scheduling, honestly

Palm Springs is the nearest town, bordering Greenacres on the east. Lake Worth Beach is roughly four to five miles from our Lake Worth Road address, Lantana about six, and Wellington and Royal Palm Beach about nine or ten. Those are rough straight-line estimates, not promises.

Traffic on Lake Worth Road, Military Trail or Southern Boulevard changes the real trip. We do not promise arrival times. We give you an honest appointment window when you call, and same-day appointments are offered when the schedule allows.

Smaller places near our coverage

Some neighbors are too small for a page of their own, but we work there too. Lake Clarke Shores, Cloud Lake and Glen Ridge sit next to Palm Springs. Hypoluxo is next to Lantana. Haverhill and Loxahatchee are near Royal Palm Beach and Wellington, and Atlantis borders Greenacres on the south.

If you live in one of them, call us and give your street address. Whether we can reach you depends on the job and on that day's schedule, and we will say so up front.

What to have ready when you call from a nearby town

Give the street address and the nearest cross street, not just the town name. Mailing names can mislead, and a Greenacres home with a Lake Worth mailing address is a good example of why.

Tell us what the problem is, whether the water is shut off and whether anyone has tried a fix. If a property manager or landlord is involved, mention it, because they may need to approve the work first.

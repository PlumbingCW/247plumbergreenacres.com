What we know about Ramblewood

Community directories describe Ramblewood as 54 single-family homes on roughly quarter-acre lots, along Ramblewood Cir and Cindi Lane just off Jog Road. The City of Greenacres runs a park at 6535 Ramblewood Cir. Those are the local facts this page relies on.

We have not verified when the homes were built, what the supply and drain pipes are made of, or whether each home is on sewer or septic. Mailing ZIP codes on listing sites do not always match city boundaries, so we use the street names instead.

The lot size is the most useful detail here, because it shapes which plumbing problems cost more.

What a quarter-acre lot changes

The paragraph below is general plumbing information. It is not a finding about specific Ramblewood homes.

On a larger lot, the water service line between the meter and the house and the sewer line between the house and the street can both run longer than on a small lot. A longer run means more pipe that can leak or crack, and more yard to dig if it does. Yards with mature trees add root pressure on drain lines. Irrigation systems and hose bibs also give water more places to leak unnoticed.

If you see a wet patch in the yard with no rain, check the meter with every fixture off. If it keeps moving, water service line repair may be the answer. For recurring drain trouble, a sewer camera inspection shows whether roots or a break are the cause.

Signs worth a call

A soggy patch in the yard with no rain

It may be an irrigation leak or a leak on the water service line. Checking the meter with everything off tells you which way to look.

Slow drains in more than one room

When several drains slow together, the main line is the likely cause. Sewer line cleaning clears it, and a camera confirms why it clogged.

Gurgling when a washing machine drains

Air moving through a partial blockage can make the noise. Do not wait for a full backup.

A water heater that takes longer to heat

It may be sediment, a failed element or a failing tank. A technician can tell which.
